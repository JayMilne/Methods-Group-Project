Group Code of Conduct:

1) Be respectful, kind, and helpful to other group members

2) Notify Members of the Group beforehand of any absences, tardies, or other reasons for an inability to participate during a meeting

3) Keep pages of the project clean and organized

4) Use Code Comments to notify other members what your code accomplishes/is meant to accomplish

5) Follow Academic Code of Conduct as set by Napier University

6) Notify group members if any issues occur.  Ask for help

7) Contribute to tasks as assigned to you.  Follow Rules 4 and 5 closely

8) Keep up with Group communications.  Keep other group members well informed

9) Show up to meetings during Lab time.  Follow Rule 2

10) Stay on task, no distractions during group work time
